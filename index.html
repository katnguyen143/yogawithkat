welcome

hi, i'm kat!

I'm a yoga teacher and acrobat based in Tsukuba, Japan. I am currently pursuing my Master's Degree in Sport Science in hopes to learn more about the science of movement and embodied discipline. Before moving to Japan, I taught summer aerial and acro camps at SANCA Seattle and an early morning hot yoga flow at Urban Yoga Spa in Capitol Hill. My teaching is rooted in the Ashtanga-Vinyasa tradition, with a welcoming approach that makes yoga feel accessible, a bit challenging, and fun.

My teaching journey began in 2024 with Outsiders, an outdoor recreation club at UC Berkeley, where I led free weekly yoga classes for BIPOC students and discovered the joy of building community through movement. In June 2025, I completed my 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training at Bali Yoga Ashram and have since taught in retreat settings and studios in Japan, Thailand, and the USA.

If you're curious about where yoga has taken me, click the see my journey button below!

current offerings

yoga and acro classes

group yoga

My group yoga classes are a fun blend of dynamic, yet accessible sequences. Whether you're looking for a place to start or to deepen your practice, you'll be supported with clear guidance, breath cues, and optional props. Expect to break a sweat and for a mix of strength-building, flexibility, and breathwork.

donation-based yoga

Yoga was never meant to be a privilege, yet financial barriers often make it feel that way. These classes are offered on a pay-what-you-can basis to make yoga more accessible, especially for BIPOC and local communities. I also occasionally host special classes where proceeds go toward community and mutual aid efforts.

private acroyoga

Learn the foundations of acro or practice flows, whips, pops, and hand-to-hand with me! As a seasoned flyer, I specialize in helping bases practice build strength, balance, and clear communication. These one-on-one sessions are recommended for beginners with some basic acro experience.
