wondering where to begin?

yoga resources ♡

I first started learning yoga through random YouTube videos. It took awhile to find styles that felt right for me, but I eventually found channels that helped me enjoy practicing yoga from home.

If you're looking for a place to start, I've gathered a few videos by category and tips for starting out your yoga practice below.

sun salutations (surya namaskar)

Sun Salutations are the foundation of many yoga practices, involving a sequence of postures that connects breath, movement, and mindfulness. Below are three traditional variations to explore: the classic Hatha Surya Namaskar, and the dynamic A and B sequences found in Vinyasa and Ashtanga styles.

Hatha Surya Namaskar

A step-by-step tutorial on the Hatha Sun Salutation sequence, perfect for beginner flows.

Surya Namaskar A

A foundational vinyasa flow linking breath and movement in a smooth, energizing rhythm.

Surya Namaskar B

A slightly longer sequence that introduces Chair Pose and Warrior I, building heat and strength.

full-body flows

These energizing flows will help you build body awareness and breath connection. One of my favorite sequences is 45-minute Power Yoga by Ally Maz!

30-minute Power Vinyasa Flow

An energizing flow focusing on core engagement and hip opening.

45-minute Power Yoga

An intermediate full-body yoga flow, great for building strength.

build strength and mobility

Once you feel comfortable with foundational movements, explore flows that develop strength and joint stability while keeping your practice grounded and balanced.

Full Body Strength & Flexibility

Dynamic sequences to support balance and endurance.

Gentle Flexibility Practice

Soft mobility work to support posture and daily comfort.

♡ kat's tips for new yogis

  • Start slow. Even 10 minutes or 3 rounds of Sun Salutations can go a long way.
  • Focus on the breath before the movements.
  • Use props such as cushions, books, or rolled towels.
  • Modify freely. Yoga meets you where you are, it is not a competition!
  • Don't worry about looking "right." Focus on how it feels.
  • Practice kindness towards yourself and acknowledge the progress you've made. Everyone's journey looks different.
