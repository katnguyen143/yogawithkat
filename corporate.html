faq

good questions

Does my team need yoga experience?

Not at all. Every pose comes with an easier option, and chair-based versions are always available.

What language are sessions taught in?

Sessions are taught in English with simple Japanese cues for movement and breath. This is a great fit for international teams.

What space do we need?

A meeting room with the chairs pushed aside works well. For mat classes, plan roughly 2 x 1 m per person.

Is partner work safe for everyone?

Yes, workshops stay low to the ground and everything is opt-in. No one is lifted without consent.

How is pricing set?

Starting prices cover up to 15 participants and travel within Tsukuba. Larger groups add about ¥5,000 per extra 10 people, and sessions in Tokyo include a travel fee. Send an inquiry and I'll put together an exact quote.
