circus & movement

Aerial, acrobatics, trampoline, and all things circus.

Camp SANCA — Summer Camps

Location: Seattle, WA
Dates: Mon–Fri, June 22 – Aug 28
Hours: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
Style: Circus Arts (partner acro, aerial, fabrics, trapeze, trampoline & more)
Role: Aerial Skills Coach

Cost $625 / week
Ring 1 · Ages 6–8 beginner

A joyful introduction to circus arts for our youngest aspiring performers! Campers build body awareness through tumbling, balancing, and movement while exploring trampoline, aerial fabric, static trapeze, object manipulation, tightwire, and German Wheel. No prior experience needed.

Ring 2 · Ages 9–12 beginner

Campers explore tumbling, trampoline, aerial fabric, static trapeze, German Wheel, object manipulation, and juggling in a supportive group environment that encourages confidence and creativity. No prior experience needed.

Circus Fantastique · Ages 7–9 intermediate

For campers ready to take their training further, with a focus on technique, safety, and careful progressions across aerial fabric, static trapeze, German Wheel, juggling, trampoline, tightwire, and more. Includes cross-training in flexibility and conditioning.
Prerequisites: 3 regular session classes or 3 weeks of camps within the last 18 months.

Advanced Camp · Ages 10–15 advanced

A rigorous, well-rounded program for experienced circus students ready for the next level. Covers aerial fabric, static trapeze, German Wheel, juggling, trampoline, tightwire, and tumbling with strong emphasis on precision and safety, plus conditioning and flexibility work.
Requirements: 3 regular session classes or 3 weeks of camps within the last 18 months.
